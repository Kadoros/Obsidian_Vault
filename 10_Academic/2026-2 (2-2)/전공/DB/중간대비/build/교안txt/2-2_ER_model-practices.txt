Recap: Entity-Relationship (ER) Model
q Describe Data as a Collection of Entities and Relationships
  ■ Entity: a “thing” or “object” that is distinguishable from others
  ■ Relationship: an association among several entities
q Focus on the Schema, Rather Than the Instances

q Process of Designing ER Schema Diagram
  ■ (1) Describe entity types and their attributes based on the data requirements

  ■ (3) Change the attributes representing relationships into relationship types

Recap: Constrains on Relationship Types
q Constraints of Relationship Types
  ■ Relationship types usually have certain constraints about participating entities
     q   e.g., the company has a rule that each employee must work for exactly one department

q Constraint 1: Cardinality Ratio
  ■ Specify the maximum number of relationship instances that an entity can participate in
  ■ Three types of cardinality ratios for a binary relationship
     q   1:1, 1:N, M:N

q Constraint 2: Participation Constraint
  ■ Specifies whether it should participate in a relationship type
  ■ Two types of a participation constraint
     q Total: every entity should participate in the relationship
     q Partial: every entity does not need to participate in the relationship

Exercise 1: ER Schema Diagram
q Designing ER Diagram for MOVIE Database
  ■ Consider a MOVIE database in which data is recorded about the movie industry.
    q MOVIE
       § Each movie is identified by (title and year of release)
       § Each has a length in minutes and a plot outline
       § Each is classified under one or more genres (e.g., horror, action, drama, …)
       § Each movie has a production company, one or more directors, and one or more actors appearing in it
    q ACTOR
       § Actors are identified by (name and date of birth) and appear in one or more movies
       § Each actor has a role in the movie and has zero or more quotable quotes to speak
    q DIRECTOR
       § Directors are identified by (name and date of birth) and direct one or more movies
       § It is possible for a director to act in a movie (including one that she/he may also direct)
    q PRODUCTION
       § Production companies are identified by (name) and each has an address
       § A production company produces one or more movies

Exercise 2: ER Schema Diagram (Cont.)
q Designing ER Diagram for CONF_REVIEW Database
  ■ The data requirements are summarized as follows:
    q AUTHOR
       § Authors of papers are uniquely identified by e-mail id and have the first and last names
       § Authors can submit one or more papers
       § An author should submit a paper to be recorded
    q PAPER
       § Each paper is assigned a unique id by the system and is described by a title, abstract, and the file name
       § Each can have multiple authors, but one of the authors should be designated as the contact author
       § A paper is assigned at least two and at most four reviewers
    q REVIEWER
       § Reviewers of papers are uniquely identified by e-mail id
       § Every reviewer has to review at least one paper to be recorded and can review many papers
       § Each reviewer has the first and last name, phone number, affiliation, and topics of interest
       § A reviewer rates each paper assigned to her/him on a scale of 1 to 10 in four evaluation categories: (1) technical
           merit, (2) readability, (3) originality, and (4) relevance to the conference.
         § Each reviewer provides an overall recommendation to each paper assigned to her/him
         § Each review contains two types of written comments: (1) one to be seen by the review committee only and (2)
           the other as feedback to author(s)
