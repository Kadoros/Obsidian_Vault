### Question 1 (5 marks)
Let $f(x) = \left(2x - \frac{1}{x^2}\right)^6$.
(a) Write down the general term in the binomial expansion of $f(x)$. [2]
(b) Find the value of the term independent of $x$ (the constant term). [3]

### Question 2 (7 marks)
Let $g(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x + 5$.
(a) Find $g'(x)$. [2]
(b) Find the gradient of the tangent to the curve of $g$ at the point where $x = 2$. [2]
(c) Find the equation of the normal to the curve of $g$ at the point where $x = 2$. Give your answer in the form $ax + by + d = 0$, where $a, b, d \in \mathbb{Z}$. [3]

### Question 3 (7 marks)
The graph of the function $h(x) = px^3 + qx^2 + 4$ has a local minimum at the point $A(2, 0)$.
(a) Find $h'(x)$. [2]
(b) Show that $12p + 4q = 0$. [2]
(c) Hence, find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [3]

### Question 4 (8 marks)
Let $f(x) = (x^2 + 2)^3$.
(a) Use the binomial theorem to expand $f(x)$ in descending powers of $x$. [3]
(b) Hence, find $f'(x)$. [2]
(c) Find the equation of the tangent to the curve $y=f(x)$ at the point where $x=-1$. [3]

### Question 5 (7 marks)
Let $f(x) = x^4 - 4x^3 + 4x^2$.
(a) Find $f'(x)$ and factorize it completely. [3]
(b) Find the $x$-coordinates of all points on the graph of $f$ where the tangent is horizontal. [2]
(c) Determine the intervals where the function $f(x)$ is strictly increasing. [2]
