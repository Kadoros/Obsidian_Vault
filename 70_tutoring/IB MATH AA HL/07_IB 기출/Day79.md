The equation $2(\log_2 x)^2 - 5\log_2 x + 2 = 0$ has two solutions, $\alpha$ and $\beta$, where $\alpha < \beta$.

  

a. State the domain restriction for the equation and solve for $\alpha$ and $\beta$. [4]

b. Consider an infinite geometric series with first term $u_1 = \alpha$ and second term $u_2 = \frac{\alpha}{\beta}$.

i. Find the common ratio $r$. [1]

ii. Explain why the sum to infinity exists. [1]

iii. Calculate the exact value of the sum to infinity, $S_{\infty}$. [2]

  


An arithmetic sequence $u_n$ has a first term $u_1 = 3$ and a common difference $d = 2$.

  

a. Write down an expression for $S_n$, the sum of the first $n$ terms, in terms of $n$. [2]

b. It is given that $4^{S_n} = \frac{(16)^{2n}}{64}$.

i. Express both sides as powers of 2 (or 4) using the laws of indices. [3]

ii. Hence, form a quadratic equation and find the value of $n$, justifying why any invalid solution is rejected. [4]

  



A geometric sequence has positive terms $v_1, v_2, v_3, \dots, v_n$ with first term $v_1 = 2$ and common ratio $r = 4$.

  

a. Express the $k$-th term, $v_k$, in the form $2^{f(k)}$. [2]

b. Consider the sum $T_n = \log_2(v_1) + \log_2(v_2) + \dots + \log_2(v_n)$.

i. Show that the terms $\log_2(v_k)$ form an arithmetic sequence, stating its first term and common difference. [3]

ii. Find the value of $n$ such that $T_n = 100$. [4]
