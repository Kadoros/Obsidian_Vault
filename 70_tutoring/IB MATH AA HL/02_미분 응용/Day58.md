![[../../../90_Resources/92_Images/Day58_20260602_2142_1.png]]**Problem 1.**

Let $f(x) = px^3 - 4x$. The point $A(2, f(2))$ lies on the graph of $f$. The gradient of the tangent to the curve at $A$ is $8$.

a. Find $f'(x)$.

b. Find the value of $p$.

**Problem 2.**

Let $g(x) = \frac{1}{2}x^4 - cx^2$. The point $P(2, g(2))$ lies on the graph of $g$. At $P$, the normal to the curve is parallel to the line $x + 4y = 8$.

a. Find $g'(x)$.

b. Find the value of $c$.

**[Optimization (Maximum/Minimum) Problems]**

**Problem 3.**

A rectangular garden is bounded on one side by an existing straight wall and on the other three sides by $40\text{ m}$ of fencing. Let $x\text{ m}$ be the length of the fencing perpendicular to the wall.

a. Show that the area of the garden, $A$, is given by $A(x) = 40x - 2x^2$.

b. Find $\frac{dA}{dx}$.

c. Use calculus to find the maximum area of the garden.

**Problem 4.**

An open-top rectangular box has a square base of side length $x\text{ cm}$. The total surface area of the box (the base and four lateral sides) is fixed at $48\text{ cm}^2$.

a. Express the height of the box in terms of $x$.

b. Express the volume, $V$, of the box as a polynomial in terms of $x$.

c. Find the value of $x$ that maximizes the volume, and calculate this maximum volume.
