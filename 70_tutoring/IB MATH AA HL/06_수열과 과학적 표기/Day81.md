1. Solve for real $x$: $4^{x+1} - 9 \cdot 2^x + 2 = 0$.
    
      
    
2. Given that $3^{x+2} - 5 \cdot 3^x = k \cdot 3^x$, find the value of the constant $k$. Hence, solve the equation $3^{x+2} - 5 \cdot 3^x = 36$.
    
      
    
3. The terms $2k - 1$, $k + 4$, and $4k - 7$ form three consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence. Find the value of $k$.
    
      
    
4. The numbers $x$, $x+3$, and $4x$ are three consecutive terms of a geometric sequence where all terms are positive. Find the exact value of $x$.
    
      
    
5. Given that $\log_2 3 = a$ and $\log_2 5 = b$, express $\log_5 12$ in terms of $a$ and $b$.
    
      
    
6. Solve for $x$: $\log_3 x + \log_9 x = 3$.
    
      
    
7. Solve for real $x$: $x^{\log_2 x} = 8x^2$.
    
      
    
8. Solve for $x$: $\log_2(x+2) + \log_2(x-2) = \log_2(3x + 6)$. State all domain restrictions and justify why any extraneous root is rejected.
    
      
    
9. Solve for real $x$: $9^x - 4 \cdot 3^{x+1} + 27 = 0$.
    
      
    
10. Simplify completely without using a calculator: $\frac{5^{x+2} - 5^x}{5^{x+1} + 5^x}$.
    
      
    
11. The terms $x-3$, $2x-1$, and $4x-2$ are three consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence. Find the common difference $d$.
    
      
    
12. Three consecutive terms of a geometric sequence of positive terms are given by $2$, $x-1$, and $x+5$. Find the value of $x$.
    
      
    
13. Given that $\log_3 2 = p$ and $\log_3 5 = q$, express $\log_2 45$ in terms of $p$ and $q$.
    
      
    
14. Solve for $x$: $\log_2 x - \log_4(x-1) = 1$.
    
      
    
15. Solve for real $x$: $x^{\log_5 x} = 25x$.
    
      
    
16. Consider the equation $\log_3(x-1) + \log_3(x+5) = \log_3(7x + 1)$. State the domain restriction on $x$, solve the equation, and state the valid solution.
