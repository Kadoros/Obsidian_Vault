**2. Fill in the blanks to complete the proof of $\log_a (xy) = \log_a x + \log_a y$:**

  

> Let $\log_a x = p$ and $\log_a y = q \implies x = \text{(a)}$, $y = \text{(b)}$.
> 
>   
> 
> Multiply both: $xy = a^{\text{(c)}}$.
> 
>   
> 
> Rewrite in log form: $\log_a (xy) = \text{(c)}$.
> 
>   
> 
> Substitute $p$ and $q$ back: $\log_a (xy) = \text{(d)}$.
