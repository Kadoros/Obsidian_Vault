**Problem 1.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 1$:

  

$$1^3 + 2^3 + 3^3 + \dots + n^3 = \left[ \frac{n(n+1)}{2} \right]^2$$

**Problem 2.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 1$:

  

$$1 + 3 + 5 + \dots + (2n-1) = n^2$$

**Problem 3.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 1$:

  

$$1 + 2 + 2^2 + 2^3 + \dots + 2^{n-1} = 2^n - 1$$

**Problem 4.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 1$:

  

$$\frac{1}{1 \cdot 2} + \frac{1}{2 \cdot 3} + \frac{1}{3 \cdot 4} + \dots + \frac{1}{n(n+1)} = \frac{n}{n+1}$$
