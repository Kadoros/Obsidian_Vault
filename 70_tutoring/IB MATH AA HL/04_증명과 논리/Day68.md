**Problem 1.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 1$:

  

$$2 + 4 + 6 + \dots + 2n = n(n+1)$$

**Problem 2.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 1$:

  

$$1 + 4 + 7 + \dots + (3n-2) = \frac{n(3n-1)}{2}$$


**Problem 3.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 1$:

  

$$3^n > 2n$$

**Problem 4.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \geq 3$:

  

$$2^n > 2n + 1$$
