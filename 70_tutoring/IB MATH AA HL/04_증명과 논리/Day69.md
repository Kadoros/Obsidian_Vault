**Problem 1.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \ge 1$:

  

$$1^2 + 2^2 + 3^2 + \dots + n^2 = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}$$

**Problem 2.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \ge 1$:

  

$$\sum_{r=1}^{n} r(r+1) = \frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{3}$$



**Problem 3.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \ge 5$:

  

$$2^n > n^2$$

**Problem 4.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \ge 4$:

  

$$n! > 2^n$$


**Problem 5.**

Prove by mathematical induction that for all integers $n \ge 2$:

$$1 + \frac{1}{2^2} + \frac{1}{3^2} + \dots + \frac{1}{n^2} \le 2 - \frac{1}{n}$$
