1. Prove that $x^4 = x^2$ for all elements $x$ in the set $S = \{-1, 0, 1\}$.

2. Prove that the sum of an even integer and an odd integer is always an odd integer.

3. Prove that the inequality $x^2 - 3x + 3 > 0$ always holds for any integer $x$ such that $0 \le x \le 3$.

4. Determine whether the following statement is true or false. If false, provide a counterexample to prove it: "All multiples of 3 are odd numbers."

5. Prove that the square of any odd integer is also an odd integer.
