1. Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the function $y = \vert{}x^2 - 4\vert{}$ and the line $y = 5$.
    
2. Find the area of the region enclosed by the parabola $y = x^2$ and the two tangent lines drawn to this parabola from the point $(0, -1)$.
    
3. The area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = -x^2 + 4x$ and the $x$-axis is bisected by the line $y = mx$ passing through the origin. Find the value of the constant $m$.
    
4. For the function $f(x) = x^3 + x^2 + x$, find the area of the region enclosed by the two curves $y = f(x)$ and $y = f^{-1}(x)$.
    
5. Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the cubic function $y = x^3 - 3x^2$ and the tangent line to this curve at its local maximum point.
    
6. Find the area of the region enclosed by the two parabolas $y = x^2$ and $y = -x^2 + 4x - 6$, and their common tangent line.
    
7. Given the function $f(x) = \int_0^x (t^2 - 4t + 3) dt$, find the area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = f'(x)$ and the $x$-axis.
    
8. Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the function $f(x) = x\vert{}x - 2\vert{}$ and the line $y = 3x$.
    
9. Find the area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^3$ and the normal line to this curve at the point $(1, 1)$.
    
10. The area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^2 + 1$ and a line passing through the point $(1, 2)$ is minimized. Find the slope of this line and the minimum area.
