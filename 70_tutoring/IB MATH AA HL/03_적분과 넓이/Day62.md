**Problem 1.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the quadratic function $y = -x^2 + 9$ and the $x$-axis.

**Problem 2.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the quadratic function $y = x^2 - 2x - 3$ and the $x$-axis.

**Problem 3.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^2$ and the line $y = 2x$.

**Problem 4.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the quadratic function $y = -x^2 + 6x$ and the $x$-axis.

**Problem 5.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the two parabolas $y = -x^2 + 3$ and $y = x^2 - 5$.

**Problem 6.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the cubic function $y = x^3 - 4x$ and the $x$-axis that lies in the first and fourth quadrants ($x \ge 0$).

**Problem 7.**

Find the total area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^2 - 4$, the $x$-axis, and the two lines $x = 0$ and $x = 3$.

**Problem 8.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the parabola $y = -x^2 + 4$ and the line $y = -x + 2$.

**Problem 9.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^2 + 1$, the tangent line to this curve at the point $(1, 2)$, and the $y$-axis ($x = 0$).

**Problem 10.**

Find the sum of the areas of the two regions enclosed by the graph of the cubic function $y = x^3 - x^2 - 2x$ and the $x$-axis.
