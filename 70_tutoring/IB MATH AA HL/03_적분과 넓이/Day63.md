**Problem 1.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the quadratic function $y = -x^2 + 4$ and the $x$-axis.

**Problem 2.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the quadratic function $y = x^2 - 4x + 3$ and the $x$-axis.

**Problem 3.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^2 - 1$ and the line $y = x + 1$.

**Problem 4.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the quadratic function $y = -x^2 + 5x$ and the $x$-axis.

**Problem 5.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the two parabolas $y = -x^2 + 4$ and $y = x^2 - 4$.

**Problem 6.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the graph of the cubic function $y = x^3 - 4x$ and the $x$-axis that lies in the fourth quadrant ($x \ge 0$, $y \le 0$).

**Problem 7.**

Find the total area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^2 - 2x$, the $x$-axis, and the two lines $x = 0$ and $x = 3$.

**Problem 8.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the parabola $y = x^2 - 3x$ and the line $y = -x$.

**Problem 9.**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the curve $y = x^2$, the tangent line to this curve at the point $(2, 4)$, and the $x$-axis.

**Problem 10.**

Find the sum of the areas of the two regions enclosed by the graph of the cubic function $y = x^3 - 3x^2 + 2x$ and the $x$-axis.
