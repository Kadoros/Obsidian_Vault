**Question 1 (5 marks)**

Let $f(x)=(x^3-\frac{1}{x^2})^5$.

(a) Write down the general term in the binomial expansion of $f(x)$. [2]

(b) Find the value of the term independent of $x$ (the constant term). [3]

**Question 2 (7 marks)**

Let $g(x)=x^3-4x^2+3x+2$.

(a) Find $g^{\prime}(x)$. [2]

(b) Find the gradient of the normal to the curve of $g$ at the point where $x=3$. [2]

(c) Find the equation of the tangent to the curve of $g$ at the point where $x=3$. Give your answer in the form $ax+by+d=0$ where $a,b,d\in\mathbb{Z}$. [3]

**Question 3 (7 marks)**

The graph of the function $h(x)=px^3+qx^2-5x+2$ has a local minimum at the point $B(1,-2)$.

(a) Find $h^{\prime}(x)$. [2]

(b) Show that $3p+2q-5=0$. [2]

(c) Hence, find the value of $p$ and the value of $q$. [3]

**Question 4 (7 marks)**

Let $f(x)=3x^4-4x^3-12x^2$.

(a) Find $f^{\prime}(x)$ and factorize it completely. [3]

(b) Find the x-coordinates of all points on the graph of $f$ where the tangent is horizontal. [2]

(c) Determine the intervals where the function $f(x)$ is strictly decreasing. [2]



**Question 5 (7 marks)**

Let $f(x) = (1 + kx)^n$, where $n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$ and $k$ is a non-zero constant.

The first three terms in the binomial expansion of $f(x)$ in ascending powers of $x$ are $1 + 36x + 540x^2$.

(a) Show that $nk = 36$. [1]

(b) Write down an equation for the coefficient of $x^2$ in terms of $n$ and $k$. [2]

(c) Hence, find the value of $n$ and the value of $k$. [4]

**Question 6 (9 marks)**

Let $g(x) = x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c$. The graph of $g$ has local extrema at $x=1$ and $x=3$.

(a) Show that $b = 9$ and find the value of $a$. [4]

(b) Given that the equation of the tangent to the curve at $x=2$ is $y = -3x + 10$, find the value of $c$. [3]

(c) Find the coordinates of the local maximum of the curve. [2]

**Question 7 (10 marks)**

Let $h(x) = x^3 - 3x^2 - x + 5$.

(a) Find the equation of the tangent to the curve $y = h(x)$ at the point $P(2, -1)$. [4]

(b) The tangent at point $P$ intersects the curve again at point $Q$. Find the coordinates of $Q$. [3]

(c) Find the equation of the normal to the curve at point $Q$. Give your answer in the form $Ax+By+D=0$ where $A,B,D\in\mathbb{Z}$. [3]
