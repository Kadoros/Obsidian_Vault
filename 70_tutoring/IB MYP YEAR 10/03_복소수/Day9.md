1. Add the real parts together and the imaginary parts together: What is $(2 + 3i) + (1 + 2i)$?
    
      
    
2. Subtract the numbers step by step: What is $(4 + 5i) - (1 + 2i)$?
    
      
    
3. On the complex plane, the real part is the x-axis and the imaginary part is the y-axis. What are the $(x, y)$ coordinates of the complex number $5 + i$?
    
      
    
4. You are at point $2 + 2i$ on the complex plane. If you move 3 units to the right (positive real direction), what is your new complex number?
    
      
    
5. You are at point $4 + 4i$. If you move 1 unit down (negative imaginary direction), what is your new complex number?
    
      
    
6. Arrow A represents $2 + i$ and Arrow B represents $1 + i$. What complex number represents the sum of Arrow A + Arrow B?
    
      
    
7. Find the result of $(5 + 3i) - (2 + 3i)$. (Notice what happens to the imaginary part!)
    
      
    
8. Add the real number $3$ to the complex number $1 + 2i$. What is the result?
    
      
    
9. Add the imaginary number $2i$ to the complex number $3 + 4i$. What is the result?
    
      
    
10. An arrow starts at the origin $(0,0)$ and points to $1 + 4i$. If you subtract $1 + 4i$ from it, what point on the complex plane do you end up at?
