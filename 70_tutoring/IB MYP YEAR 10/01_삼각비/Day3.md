**Problem 1.** In a right triangle $ABC$ with $\angle A = 90^\circ$, let $H$ be the foot of the perpendicular from vertex $A$ to the hypotenuse $BC$. If $AB = 5$, $AC = 12$, and $\angle BAH = x$, what is the value of $\sin x + \cos x$?

**Problem 2.** In a right triangle $ABC$ with $\angle A = 90^\circ$, let $H$ be the foot of the perpendicular from vertex $A$ to the hypotenuse $BC$. If $AB = 8$, $BC = 17$, and $\angle CAH = y$, what is the value of $\tan y$?

**Problem 3.** In a right triangle $ABC$ with $\angle C = 90^\circ$, if $BC = 7$ and $AC = 24$, what is the value of $\sin A + \sin B$?

**Problem 4.** In a right triangle $ABC$ with $\angle B = 90^\circ$, if $AB = 9$ and $BC = 12$, what is the value of $\cos A \times \tan C$?

_(Common condition for Problems 5-6)_ Consider a quarter circle in the coordinate plane centered at the origin with a radius of 1. Let $x^\circ$ be the acute angle formed by the line segment $OA$ connecting the origin $O$ to a point $A$ on the quarter circle and the positive $x$-axis. Let $B$ be the foot of the perpendicular from point $A$ to the $x$-axis, and let $D$ be the point $(1, 0)$. A vertical line tangent to the quarter circle at point $D$ intersects the extension of the line segment $OA$ at point $C$.

**Problem 5.** In the quarter circle described above, which line segment represents the value of $\cos x^\circ$?

**Problem 6.** In the quarter circle described above, which line segment represents the value of $\tan x^\circ$?


**Problem 7.** In a quarter circle with a radius of 1, given that $\angle AOB = 42^\circ$, the coordinates of point $A$ on the quarter circle are $(0.74, 0.67)$, and the coordinates of point $C$ on the line $x=1$ are $(1, 0.90)$. Find the value of $\sin 42^\circ + \cos 42^\circ$.

**Problem 8.** Under the conditions of Problem 7, find the value of $\tan 42^\circ$.


**Problem 9.** Evaluate the following expression:

$$\sin 30^\circ \times \cos 60^\circ + \tan 45^\circ \times \sin 90^\circ$$

**Problem 10.** Which of the following is **incorrect**?

① $\sin 0^\circ + \cos 90^\circ = 0$

② $\tan 45^\circ - \sin 90^\circ = 0$

③ $\sin 30^\circ + \cos 60^\circ = 1$

④ $\cos 0^\circ \times \tan 0^\circ = 1$

⑤ $\sin 90^\circ \times \cos 0^\circ = 1$
