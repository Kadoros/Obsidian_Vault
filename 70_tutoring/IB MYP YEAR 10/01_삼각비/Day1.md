- **Problem 1:** In a right triangle, the two shorter legs are $a = 3$ and $b = 4$. Use the Pythagorean theorem to find the length of the hypotenuse ($c$).
    
- **Problem 2:** A right triangle has a hypotenuse of 13 and one leg of 5. Find the length of the other leg.
    
- **Problem 3:** In the triangle from Problem 1, let $\theta$ be the angle opposite the side of length 3. Find the values of $sin~\theta$, $cos~\theta$, and $tan~\theta$.
    
- **Problem 4:** In a right triangle, one angle is 45°, and the adjacent side is 10 cm. Find the length of the hypotenuse.
    
- **Problem 5:** An angle in a right triangle is 30°, and the opposite side is 7 cm. Calculate the length of the hypotenuse ($k$) using the given equation $sin(30^{\circ}) = \frac{7}{k}$.
    
- **Problem 6:** In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is 23 m, and one angle is 16°. Find the length of the adjacent side.
    
- **Problem 7:** An angle in a right triangle is 60°, and the adjacent side is 5 cm. Which trigonometric ratio should you use to find the length of the opposite side? Use that ratio to calculate the length of the opposite side.
    
- **Problem 8:** In a right triangle, the hypotenuse is 15 m, and one angle is 45°. Find the length of the side opposite this 45° angle.
    
- **Problem 9:** An angle in a right triangle is 30°, and the opposite side is 9 cm. Set up an equation to find the length of the adjacent side, and then calculate its length.
    
- **Problem 10:** A ladder is leaning against a wall. The angle the ladder makes with the ground is 60°, and the base of the ladder is 5 m away from the wall. Draw a simple diagram of the situation, and use $cos(60^{\circ})$ to find the total length of the ladder.
