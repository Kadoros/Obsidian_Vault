3. A pole of length $4\text{ m}$ is placed vertically on the ground, and its shadow is $4\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$ long. At the exact same time, find the length of the shadow cast by a $6\text{ m}$ tall tree standing vertically on the ground.


4. A pendulum of length $30\text{ cm}$ swings to point $A$, making a $40^\circ$ angle with the vertical center axis. Compared to when the pendulum is at its lowest point $O$, how many $\text{cm}$ higher is the bob when it is at point $A$? (Assume the size of the bob is negligible.)

① $30 \sin 40^\circ \text{ cm}$

② $30 \cos 40^\circ \text{ cm}$

③ $30(1 - \sin 40^\circ) \text{ cm}$

④ $30(1 - \cos 40^\circ) \text{ cm}$

⑤ $30 \tan 40^\circ \text{ cm}$

5. A person is flying a kite in an open field. The taut string is $50\text{ m}$ long, and the angle it makes with the horizontal ground is $60^\circ$. If the person holding the string has their hands positioned $1.5\text{ m}$ above the ground, what is the total height of the kite from the ground?


6. A $5\text{ m}$ long pole is leaning, making a $60^\circ$ angle with the ground. Sunlight is shining down, making a $45^\circ$ angle with the ground. Find the length of the pole's shadow cast on the ground. (Assume the direction the pole is leaning and the direction of the sunlight are on the same vertical plane.)

7. Due to a strong typhoon, a perfectly upright tree broke in the middle, and its top touched the ground. The broken, leaning part makes a $30^\circ$ angle with the ground, and the distance from the point where the top touches the ground to the base of the tree is $9\text{ m}$. Find the original height of the tree before it broke.

8. From a point on flat ground, the angle of elevation to the top of a tower is $30^\circ$. After walking $20\text{ m}$ in a straight line directly towards the tower, the angle of elevation becomes $45^\circ$. Assuming the observer's eye level is at the ground level, find the height of the tower.
