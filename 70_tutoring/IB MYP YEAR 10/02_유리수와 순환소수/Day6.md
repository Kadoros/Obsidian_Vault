1. When the fraction $\frac{5}{27}$ is expressed as a decimal, find the digit in the 40th decimal place.
    
2. When a natural number $A$ is multiplied by each of the fractions $\frac{5}{84}$ and $\frac{11}{150}$, both fractions become terminating decimals. Find the smallest possible natural number $A$.
    
3. When the fraction $\frac{x}{2^3 \times 3 \times 5}$ is reduced to its simplest form, it becomes $\frac{7}{y}$, and when expressed as a decimal, it becomes a terminating decimal. If $x$ is a natural number greater than 20 and less than 40, find the value of $x+y$.
    
4. For a natural number $a$ less than or equal to 10, when the fraction $\frac{3}{2^2 \times a}$ is expressed as a decimal, it becomes a repeating decimal. Find the sum of all possible values of the natural number $a$.
    
5. When expressing a fraction in its simplest form as a decimal, Jimin misread the denominator and wrote it as $0.5\dot{8}$, and Jungkook misread the numerator and wrote it as $0.\dot{2}\dot{1}$. Express the original fraction correctly as a repeating decimal.
    
6. For two natural numbers $a$ and $b$ that satisfy $1.\dot{5} \times \frac{a}{b} = 0.\dot{7}$, find the value of $b-a$. (Assume $a$ and $b$ are relatively prime.)
    
7. For single-digit natural numbers $a$ and $b$, if $0.\dot{a}\dot{b} + 0.\dot{b}\dot{a} = 0.\dot{8}$, find the value of $a+b$.
    
8. When expressing the repeating decimal $x = 1.2\dot{4}$ as a fraction, write the most convenient equation needed in the form of $ax - bx$, and then find the value of $90x - 10$.
    
9. Find the difference between the largest and smallest numbers among $0.\dot{3}$, $0.3\dot{4}$, $\frac{1}{3}$, and $\frac{4}{11}$, and express the result as a fraction in its simplest form.
    
10. Calculate the value of $0.\dot{8} - 0.1\dot{3} \div 0.\dot{2}$ and express the result as a repeating decimal.
