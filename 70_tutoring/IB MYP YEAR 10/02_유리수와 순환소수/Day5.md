**1.** Which of the following statements about rational numbers are true? Choose all that apply.

- A. Numbers that can be expressed in the form $\frac{a}{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are integers and $b \neq 0$, are called rational numbers.
    
- B. Decimals are divided into terminating and non-terminating decimals, and all non-terminating decimals are rational numbers.
    
- C. Non-terminating, non-repeating decimals are not rational numbers.
    

**2.** Find the repeating block (the repetend) for each of the following repeating decimals, and express the decimal simply using dot notation.

- (1) $0.353535\cdots$
    
- (2) $2.1454545\cdots$
    

**3.** Which of the following fractions can be expressed as terminating decimals? Choose all that apply.

- (1) $\frac{9}{40}$
    
- (2) $\frac{7}{2^2 \times 3}$
    
- (3) $\frac{21}{2 \times 3 \times 5}$
    
- (4) $\frac{11}{14}$
    

**4.** When the fraction $\frac{x}{2^2 \times 3 \times 5}$ is expressed as a decimal, it becomes a terminating decimal. Find the smallest natural number $x$ that satisfies this condition.

**5.** Find all natural numbers $x$ less than or equal to 10 such that the fraction $\frac{13}{2 \times x}$ is a non-terminating repeating decimal rather than a terminating decimal.

**6.** When the fraction $\frac{5}{27}$ is expressed as a decimal, find the digit in the 40th decimal place.

**7.** For the repeating decimal $1.2\dot{3}4\dot{5}$, let $a$ be the digit in the 50th decimal place and $b$ be the digit in the 100th decimal place. Find the value of $a+b$.

**8.** To convert the repeating decimal $0.\dot{7}\dot{2}$ into a fraction, we set $x = 0.727272\cdots$. Write the most appropriate algebraic expression to use for this conversion (e.g., $10x - x$, $100x - 10x$, etc.).

**9.** Express the following repeating decimals as fractions in simplest form.

- (1) $0.4\dot{6}$
    
- (2) $1.\dot{0}1\dot{2}$
    

**10.** For two natural numbers $a$ and $b$, the fraction $\frac{a}{2 \times 5^2 \times b}$ becomes a terminating decimal when expressed as a decimal. If $b=7$, find the smallest two-digit natural number $a$ that satisfies this condition.
