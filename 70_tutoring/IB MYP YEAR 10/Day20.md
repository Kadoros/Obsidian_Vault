1. An equilateral triangle has an area of $25\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^2$. Find the length of each side.
    
      
    
2. In a right-angled triangle $ACD$, a line segment $BE$ is parallel to $CD$ with $B$ on $AC$ and $E$ on $AD$. The length of $[BC]$ is equal to $[BE]$, $[AC] = 12\text{ cm}$, and $[CD]$ is $2\text{ cm}$ more than twice the length of $[BE]$. Find the length of $[BE]$.
    
      
    
3. A lecture hall has a central seating section with $n$ seats per row, flanked on both sides by aisles and outer sections with $5$ seats per row each. The number of rows in the hall is $6$ less than the total number of seats per row. If the hall has a total of $160$ seats, find the value of $n$.
    
      
    
4. The sum of a positive number and its reciprocal is $2\frac{9}{10}$. Find the number.
    
      
    
5. If $x^2 - 4x + 1 = 0$, find the value of $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$.
    
      
    
6. A rectangular piece of sheet metal is $16\text{ cm}$ long and $12\text{ cm}$ wide. An open rectangular box with a base area of $60\text{ cm}^2$ is formed by cutting equal squares from all four corners and folding up the sides. Find the side length of the squares cut from each corner.
    
      
    
7. If $x^2 - 5x - 1 = 0$, find the value of $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$.
    
      
    
8. A right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of length $13\text{ cm}$. If its perimeter is $30\text{ cm}$, find the lengths of the two shorter sides by setting up and solving a quadratic equation.
    
      
    
9. A rectangular swimming pool is $12\text{ m}$ long and $8\text{ m}$ wide. It is surrounded by a paved path of uniform width $x\text{ m}$. If the area of the paved path alone is equal to the area of the pool itself, find the exact width of the path $x$ in simplest radical form.
