**Problem 2: Optimization (Maximum Volume)** An open-top rectangular box has a square base of side length $x\text{ cm}$. The total surface area of the box (the base and four lateral sides) is fixed at $108\text{ cm}^2$.

1. Express the height $h$ of the box in terms of $x$.
    
2. Express the volume $V$ of the box as a polynomial in terms of $x$.
    
3. Find the value of $x$ that maximizes the volume, and calculate this maximum volume.
