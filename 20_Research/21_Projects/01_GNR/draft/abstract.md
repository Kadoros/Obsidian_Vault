# importance

Most neural operator methods assume access to large amounts of data to learn system dynamics from scratch. However, real-world engineering typically operates in a low-data regime where the governing PDEs are known but incomplete (i.e., model-form error), and real observations are scarce. In such settings, existing approaches struggle because they entangle the known governing laws with the unknown discrepancies. This forces the model to expend its limited representational capacity and scarce training data on approximating already-known physics, rather than isolating the unmodeled phenomena.
